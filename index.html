About me

I am a graduate student pursuing M.Com, with a background in Commerce and software programming from NavGurukul, Raigarh. I have a strong interest in technology, data analysis, programming, and understanding how things work.

I enjoy exploring new subjects, taking on challenges, and learning by doing. I have been developing my skills in Python, Django, HTML, CSS. I enjoy working on projects that allow me to apply what I learn. Currently, I am developing Project Unheard, an anonymous writing feedback platform.

Outside academics and technology, reading is something I genuinely enjoy. I like reading novels, manga, and webtoons, particularly fantasy, historical fiction, and stories with interesting plots and complex characters. I enjoy exploring different perspectives through the stories I read.

I consider myself curious and self-driven, and I like exploring areas beyond my academic background. My long-term goal is to build a career in technology, strengthen my analytical and problem-solving abilities, and continue challenging myself to learn and grow. I am also interested in understanding the connection between technology, business, and data.
